Free qualitative coding software for students

Short answer. Yes. Taguette and QualCoder are free and open source. QualCanvas has a Free plan at $0 with no time limit, limited to 2 canvases, 5 transcripts per canvas and 10 codes. Student plans start at $5 a month. Free tiers are tight for a full thesis.

Cheap, not free

  • QualCanvas Student: $5 a month or $48 a year with a verified academic email. 5 canvases, AI auto-code, all 13 analysis tools.
  • Dedoose Student: $12.95 per active month, with a free first month (Dedoose pricing page, checked 3 November 2026).
  • Delve: month-to-month with a 14-day free trial; price not shown on its home page.

Which to choose

A small pilot with a handful of interviews can fit inside the QualCanvas Free limits or in Taguette. For a whole thesis, 10 codes and 5 transcripts per canvas on QualCanvas Free is tight, so Taguette, QualCoder or a licence from your university are better free answers, or move to the Student plan. Whatever you use, export regularly, for example to .qdpx, so you can move.

Frequently asked questions

Is there free qualitative coding software?

Yes. Taguette and QualCoder are free and open source, and QualCanvas has a Free plan at $0 with no time limit (2 canvases, 5 transcripts per canvas, 10 codes). Checked 3 November 2026.

Is the QualCanvas Free plan enough for a thesis?

Usually not for a full thesis, because it allows 10 codes and 5 transcripts per canvas. It is enough to learn the tool and to code a small pilot. The Student plan is $5 a month with a verified academic email.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan. Nothing you made is deleted.
