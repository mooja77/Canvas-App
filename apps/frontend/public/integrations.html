Imports and exports

Files in, files out

QualCanvas moves data by file, not by account connection. Nothing here asks for access to your Zoom, Slack, or Google account — you export from the other tool and upload, or you export from QualCanvas and take it wherever you like. For research data under an ethics approval, that is usually the point.

Transcript import

  • Caption files (.vtt / .srt)The usual export from Zoom, Otter, Teams and YouTube. Cue numbers, timestamps and markup are stripped; <v Speaker> voice tags become "Speaker:" labels; repeated rolling-caption lines are collapsed.
  • Word (.docx)Upload the file as-is. Text is extracted for coding; formatting, comments and tracked changes are not carried over.
  • Plain text (.txt)Anything your tool can export as text, including Descript and Rev transcript exports.
  • CSVOne row per response — useful for survey open-ends and other short-answer data.

Project interchange

  • REFI-QDA (.qdpx) importReads the open REFI-QDA interchange format. Codes and their nesting, text sources and coded text segments are brought across. Tested against real exports from NVivo 12 (Windows) and ATLAS.ti 8 (macOS).
  • REFI-QDA (.qdpx) exportTake your project back out the same way. QDPX is an open standard rather than a QualCanvas format, so this is your exit route as much as your entry route.

What does not come across. Codes, their nesting, text sources and coded text segments transfer both ways. Cases, variables, sets, memos, graphs, links, uncoded quotations and non-text sources such as audio, video and PDF do not — the import counts each of these and tells you exactly what it dropped, rather than reporting a clean success. Treat a transfer as a migration to check over rather than a lossless copy, and keep your original project file until you have.

Export

  • CSVCodebook and coded-segment export, for mixed-methods triangulation with quantitative tools.
  • PNGCanvas image export for slides, posters and supervision meetings.
  • HTML / MarkdownReadable write-ups of codes and their coded excerpts, ready to paste into a document.
  • QDPXThe full project, as above.

CSV export is on every plan including Free. PNG, HTML, Markdown and QDPX are on the paid plans.
