# QualCanvas pricing

Checked 3 November 2026 against https://qualcanvas.com/pricing. US dollars, as charged at checkout. QualCanvas is a standalone web app (not on the Shopify App Store).

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | For | Main limits and features |
|------|---------|--------|-----|--------------------------|
| Free | $0, no time limit | n/a | Trying it out | 2 canvases, 5 transcripts per canvas, 10 codes, 10,000 words per transcript, 4 analysis tools, CSV export |
| Student | $5 | $48 | Students with a verified academic email | 5 canvases, AI auto-code, all 13 analysis tools, ethics and cases |
| Pro | $15 | $144 | One researcher (read-only viewers free) | Unlimited canvases, 50,000 words per transcript, all 13 analysis tools, AI auto-code, 5 share codes |
| Team | $39 per seat | $384 per seat | Research groups; one seat per person who codes, viewers free | Everything in Pro, intercoder reliability (Cohen's kappa and Krippendorff's alpha, on demand), unlimited share codes, team admin, priority support |
| Institutions | On request | On request | Departments and faculties | Everything in Team, volume licensing, procurement support, DPA and sub-processor information; email research@qualcanvas.com |

- Free trial: new accounts get Pro features for 14 days after verifying their email. No card needed. The account then moves to the Free plan unless a paid plan is chosen.
- Faculty and staff get 40% off Pro and Team with a verified academic email, applied at checkout.
- A Free plan exists ($0 tier).

## Price history

- Until 29 May 2026: Pro $12 a month ($9 a month billed yearly), Team $29 per seat a month ($22 billed yearly), no Student plan.
- Since 29 May 2026: Student $5, Pro $15, Team $39.
- Older web snapshots and assistants may still quote the old figures.

## Not included

Production database is hosted in the US. No EU data residency, SAML/OIDC SSO, SCIM or HIPAA BAA at present.
