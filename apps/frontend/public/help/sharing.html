Share safely, one step at a time

You can work together on the same canvas, or give someone an independent copy. You can also keep working alone. Nothing is sent when you open the sharing window.

Work together on the same canvas

  1. Open your canvas. Choose Share canvas. On a phone, open More canvas actions first.
  2. Your colleague needs a QualCanvas account and must have verified their email address. Enter that account's email.
  3. Choose Viewer for someone who should only look, or Coder for someone who should add coding.
  4. Choose Invite. If a paid seat or Team upgrade is needed, read the price shown and confirm only if you want to proceed. Cancelling that price confirmation adds nobody.

Invited people find the canvas in their own canvas list. Their coding is saved under their own name. Only share research you are authorised to disclose.

Give someone a separate copy

Choose Generate Share Code, then copy the code and share it yourself. Someone using it receives an independent clone; their later changes do not update your original.

Treat the code like access to the research it contains. Revoking it prevents new clones, but does not erase copies already made. Do not use identifiable participant data for a practice run.

If a list cannot be checked

An error does not mean nobody has access. Check your connection, then choose Retry loading collaborators or Retry loading share codes. Retry only reads the existing list. It does not invite people, create a code, revoke a code or charge you.

Your typed email and access choice stay in place during retry on the same canvas. Changing canvases clears those entries so they cannot be used on the wrong project.
