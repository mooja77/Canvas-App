# Pilot recruitment

Goal: five completed sessions in the next two weeks, at least three from people who have never opened QualCanvas.
Warm starts first (existing accounts with real interview work), then their colleagues.

Who to ask first:

1. Billy Goodwin, UCC (Team account, comped). Ask him to do one session himself and to forward the note to two
   postgraduates who code interviews.
2. Jody (WISESHIFT). She has coded work in the product already; ask for one session and one introduction to a
   colleague on a different project.
3. The four free accounts from May and June who never came back (see `/admin`, Users). Different email, see below.

Session logistics: 20 to 30 minutes on a video call, screen shared, synthetic material only, no recording unless
they agree separately. Facilitator script is in `FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md`; observation sheet is `SCORECARD.csv`.

---

## Email 1: warm contact (Billy, Jody)

Subject: 30 minutes to break QualCanvas?

Hi [name],

You have used QualCanvas for real coding work, which makes you the person I most want to watch use it.

I am running a small usability pilot: five short tasks (create a project, add a transcript, code a passage, write a
memo and run one analysis, export) on a video call, 20 to 30 minutes, using made-up text so nothing sensitive is
involved. I stay quiet and take notes. You say what you expect to happen and what confuses you. Needing help is
useful evidence, not a failure.

Would you have a slot in the next two weeks? Reply with two or three times that suit and I will send a link.

If you know one or two postgraduates who code interviews and have never seen the tool, I would value an introduction
even more than your own session. First-time eyes find what I cannot.

Thanks,
John
