# Pilot recruitment

Goal: five completed sessions in the next two weeks, at least three from people who have never opened QualCanvas.
Warm starts first (existing accounts with real interview work), then their colleagues.

Who to ask first. Addresses are deliberately not recorded here: read them from `/admin` > Users, which is the
only place this personal data belongs. Descriptions below match rows in that table.

1. The two comped Team accounts (the UCC researcher and his second account; the WISESHIFT researcher with seven
   canvases). Both have coded real work. Ask each for one session and, more valuable, one introduction to a
   colleague who has never opened the tool.
2. The five lapsed free accounts from April to May. Use email 2.
3. Anyone the first group introduces. Use email 3.

**What the account data already tells us, and what the pilot must therefore probe.** Every lapsed account created
one or two canvases and then never returned; not one is recorded as active after signup day. So the drop-off is
not at signup or at project creation, it is somewhere between an empty canvas and a first coded excerpt. Task 3
of the study (code a passage) is the one to watch hardest, and the neutral prompt to have ready is "what were you
expecting to happen when you clicked that?" One person signed up twice the same day on two different addresses,
which usually means a failed login or verification loop; if that participant answers, ask what happened the first
time before running the tasks.

Their institutions also say something about who is finding the product: a Japanese university, a Pakistani
institute, a UK student address, and a German personal address. None are the Irish academic market the pricing
now targets. Worth holding in mind when reading the results.

Session logistics: 20 to 30 minutes on a video call, screen shared, synthetic material only, no recording unless
they agree separately. Facilitator script is in `FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md`; observation sheet is `SCORECARD.csv`.

## Email 1: warm contact (the two Team accounts)

Subject: 30 minutes to break QualCanvas?

Hi [name],

You have used QualCanvas for real coding work, which makes you the person I most want to watch use it.

I am running a small usability pilot: five short tasks (create a project, add a transcript, code a passage, write a
memo and run one analysis, export) on a video call, 20 to 30 minutes, using made-up text so nothing sensitive is
involved. I stay quiet and take notes. You say what you expect to happen and what confuses you. Needing help is
useful evidence, not a failure.

Would you have a slot in the next two weeks? Reply with two or three times that suit and I will send a link.

If you know one or two postgraduates who code interviews and have never seen the tool, I would value an introduction
even more than your own session. First-time eyes find what I cannot.

Thanks,
John

## Email 2: cold or lapsed account

Subject: You started a project in QualCanvas and stopped. What happened?

Hi [name],

You created a QualCanvas account in [month], started a project, and then did not come back. That is on me, not you,
and I would like to understand where it lost you.

I am running a short usability pilot: five first-use tasks on a video call, 20 to 30 minutes, with made-up interview
text. No preparation, no account needed beyond the one you have, and you can stop at any point. I watch, you talk.

If that is something you could do in the next two weeks, reply with a couple of times. If not, a one-line reply
saying what you were hoping the tool would do would still help me.

Thanks,
John Moore
QualCanvas
